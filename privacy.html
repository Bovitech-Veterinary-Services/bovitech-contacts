Bovitech Contacts — Privacy policy

Bovitech Contacts is an internal app used only by staff of Bovitech Veterinary Services, 317 Gull Rock Road, Kalgan WA 6330.

What it stores

Client and business contact details (names, phone numbers, email and postal addresses, farm locations, PICs, herd/flock IDs and notes) that Bovitech staff enter for running the practice.

Where it is stored

On the staff device using the app, and in the practice Google Drive account. When enabled, names, phone numbers, email addresses, business names and a photo are copied to the practice Google Contacts account so staff phones can identify callers.

Google data

The app uses Google sign-in only to read and write its own data file in the practice Google Drive (drive.file) and to update the practice Google Contacts. It does not use Google data for advertising, does not sell or share it, and does not send it to anyone other than Google. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
